# Decision Frameworks

## Decision Matrix

Use when comparing options against weighted criteria.

Steps:

1. Define decision options.
2. Define criteria.
3. Confirm weights.
4. Score each option.
5. Identify sensitivity: which assumption could flip the decision?

Do not score without criteria and evidence. Ask for weights or mark them as assumptions.

## Risk Matrix

Use for project, launch, strategy, or operational risk.

Dimensions:

- Probability.
- Impact.
- Detectability.
- Mitigation.
- Owner.

Output:

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Early signal | Mitigation | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Premortem

Use before launch or major decisions.

Prompt: "Assume this failed badly 6 months later. What caused it?"

Steps:

1. List plausible failure modes.
2. Cluster by root cause.
3. Identify early warning signals.
4. Define preventive actions.

## Delphi

Use when expert judgment is needed and direct debate may bias results.

Process:

1. Define the question.
2. Collect anonymous expert estimates.
3. Summarize convergence and disagreement.
4. Run another round if needed.
5. Record final consensus and dissent.

Do not claim consensus unless the process actually happened.

## DACI / RACI

Use when roles and decision rights are unclear.

DACI:

- Driver.
- Approver.
- Contributors.
- Informed.

RACI:

- Responsible.
- Accountable.
- Consulted.
- Informed.
