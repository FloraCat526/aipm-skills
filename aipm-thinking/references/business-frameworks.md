# Business Frameworks

## Business Model Canvas

Use when shaping or evaluating a business model.

Blocks:

- Customer segments.
- Value propositions.
- Channels.
- Customer relationships.
- Revenue streams.
- Key resources.
- Key activities.
- Key partners.
- Cost structure.

Ask:

- Who pays, who uses, and who decides?
- What painful problem is solved?
- How does the product reach customers?
- What costs scale with usage or delivery?
- What must be true for the model to work?

## Value Proposition Canvas

Use when clarifying product-market fit.

Customer profile:

- Jobs.
- Pains.
- Gains.

Value map:

- Products and services.
- Pain relievers.
- Gain creators.

Output should explicitly connect each pain/gain to a product capability.

## Unit Economics

Use when commercial viability depends on cost and revenue.

Key metrics:

- CAC.
- ARPU / ACV.
- Gross margin.
- Payback period.
- Retention / churn.
- Cost per AI request if AI is involved.

Do not invent numbers. If unknown, ask for current estimates or mark `待确认`.
